# Tomorrow Health

> Tomorrow Health is an AI-powered platform for home-based care that connects providers, payors, and durable medical equipment (DMEPOS) suppliers, using intelligent automation to simplify ordering, routing, and reviewing home healthcare services.

Tomorrow Health's "Horizon Suite" streamlines home-based care with AI Ordering (converting uploads into draft orders), AI Fax Intake (turning faxed documents into clean digital orders), and THEA (Tomorrow Health Expert Assistant) for routine inquiries. The company serves providers, suppliers, health plans, and patients. It is headquartered at 107 Greenwich St, New York, NY 10006, and is backed by a16z, Bond Capital, and Obvious Ventures.

There is no public developer API, SDK, or API documentation. The customer application is a private, authenticated single-page app. This llms.txt is generated by the API Evangelist enrichment pipeline from public company surfaces.

## Audiences

- [For Providers](https://home.tomorrowhealth.com/providers): Ordering and coordination for referring clinicians.
- [For Suppliers](https://home.tomorrowhealth.com/suppliers): DMEPOS supplier workflow and routing.
- [For Health Plans](https://home.tomorrowhealth.com/health-plans): Payor-side review and management.
- [For Patients](https://home.tomorrowhealth.com/patients): Patient-facing home health care information.
